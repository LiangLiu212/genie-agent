# Gold-197 (¹⁹⁷Au)

Heavy nuclear target, A = 197.

## Measurements

### (e,e'p) quasi-elastic at JLab Hall C (nucl-ex/0303011)

- ≈ 200 mg/cm² solid target (thickness known to ≈ 0.1%) at [[jlab-hall-c]];
  a Perey factor (β = 0.85) was applied for Au in the [[spectral-function]]
  modeling (nucl-ex/0303011). Channel: [[em-qe]].
- Nuclear [[nuclear-transparency]] (Table III; statistical errors in
  parentheses) (nucl-ex/0303011):
  - Q² = 0.64 (θ_e forward) 0.38(0.01); (θ_e backward) 0.43(0.01)
  - Q² = 1.28: 0.32(0.01)
  - Q² = 1.80 (θ_e forward) 0.29(0.01); (backward point not reported)
  - Q² = 3.25: 0.28(0.01)
- Transparency PWIA correlation factor applied: 1.32 ± 0.08 (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Momentum distributions integrated over 0 < E_m < 80 MeV (nucl-ex/0303011).
- The gold [[longitudinal-transverse-separation]] (at Q² = 0.64 (GeV/c)²) is
  among the first such L–T data on a heavy nucleus; excess transverse strength
  at Q² = 0.64 (GeV/c)², much reduced at 1.8 (GeV/c)² (nucl-ex/0303011).

Source: [[source/nucl-ex_0303011]].
