# Polystyrene scintillator (CH)

The active scintillator (≈CH) that forms the MINERvA tracker — both the
detection medium and the high-statistics **reference target** to which passive
nuclei ([[c12]], [[o16]], [[fe56]], [[pb]]) are compared (2503.15047).

## Measurements

### ν_μ CCQE-like at MINERvA (2503.15047)

- Tracker region: 124 contiguous polystyrene scintillator planes at [[minerva]]
  (2503.15047). Channel: [[ccqe]].
- Listed in the statistics table as Z = 7, N = 6 (an effective scintillator
  composition, not pure carbon); 218,000 events after cuts, 60% purity — the
  highest-statistics target (2503.15047).
  > [!note] The Z = 7, N = 6 entry is the source's effective-CH value; pure
  > carbon would be Z = 6. Flagged in the paper's open questions.
- Serves as the denominator for the cross-section **ratios to CH** (scaled by
  target neutron number); flux and hadron/muon-reconstruction uncertainties
  largely cancel in these ratios (2503.15047). Ratios per neutron are consistent
  with unity for [[o16]] (water) and [[c12]], but deviate clearly for [[fe56]]
  and [[pb]] ([[fsi]]) (2503.15047).
- Measured vs [[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]] variables and muon/proton
  kinematics.
