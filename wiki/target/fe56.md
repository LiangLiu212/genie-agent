# Iron-56 (⁵⁶Fe)

Medium-mass nuclear target, A = 56.

## Measurements

### ν_μ CCQE-like vs muon kinematics at MINERvA (2301.02272)

- Passive iron target at [[minerva]], NuMI ME beam (peak E_ν ≈ 6.5 GeV);
  92,000 selected interactions after background subtraction (2301.02272).
  Channel: [[ccqe]] (muon-only signal, no proton requirement).
- **Fe/CH cross-section ratio per nucleon ≈ 1.4–1.5**, while MINERvA's
  underlying [[genie]] model (not tuned to Fe/Pb) predicts ≈ 1.2 (2301.02272) —
  the data sit above the prediction. Like [[pb]], **the overall level and the
  P_T shape for Fe are not reproduced by current generators** (2301.02272).
- Part of the evidence for A-scaling that is not constant over the momentum
  transferred to the nucleus (2301.02272). See [[fsi]].

Source: [[source/2301.02272]].

### ν_μ CCQE-like at MINERvA (2503.15047)

- Passive iron target at [[minerva]], NuMI ME beam (⟨E_ν⟩ ∼ 6 GeV); 8,577 events
  after cuts, 47% purity (2503.15047). Channel: [[ccqe]]. Measured vs
  [[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]] and muon/proton kinematics.
- Cross-section **ratio to [[ch]] per neutron deviates clearly from unity**
  (unlike light [[c12]] and [[o16]]), and the δP_T distribution shifts to higher
  values — nuclear effects / [[fsi]] grow with A (2503.15047). The deviation is
  smaller than for [[pb]].
- Source: [[source/2503.15047]].

### (e,e'p) quasi-elastic at JLab Hall C (nucl-ex/0303011)

- ≈ 200 mg/cm² solid target (thickness known to ≈ 0.1%) at [[jlab-hall-c]];
  a Perey factor (β = 0.85) was applied for Fe in the [[spectral-function]]
  modeling (nucl-ex/0303011). Channel: [[em-qe]].
- Nuclear [[nuclear-transparency]] (Table III; statistical errors in
  parentheses) (nucl-ex/0303011):
  - Q² = 0.64 (θ_e forward) 0.47(0.01); (θ_e backward) 0.54(0.01)
  - Q² = 1.28: 0.44(0.01)
  - Q² = 1.80 (θ_e forward) 0.40(0.01); Q² = 1.83 (θ_e backward) 0.44(0.01)
  - Q² = 3.25: 0.42(0.01)
- Transparency PWIA correlation factor applied: 1.26 ± 0.08 (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Momentum distributions integrated over 0 < E_m < 80 MeV (nucl-ex/0303011).
- The iron [[longitudinal-transverse-separation]] (at Q² = 0.64 and
  1.8 (GeV/c)²) is among the first such L–T data on a medium nucleus; excess
  transverse strength at Q² = 0.64 (GeV/c)², much reduced at 1.8 (GeV/c)²
  (nucl-ex/0303011).

Source: [[source/nucl-ex_0303011]].
