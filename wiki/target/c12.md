# Carbon-12 (¹²C)

Light nuclear target, A = 12.

## Measurements

### ν_μ CCQE-like vs muon kinematics at MINERvA (2301.02272)

- Passive carbon target at [[minerva]], NuMI ME beam (peak E_ν ≈ 6.5 GeV); used
  as a **control region**, 25,000 selected interactions after background
  subtraction (2301.02272). Channel: [[ccqe]] (muon-only signal, no proton
  requirement).
- Cross-section ratios of C (and [[o16]] water) to [[ch]] **show no significant
  deviation from unity** and stay roughly constant with increasing P_∥ — simple
  scaling holds for the light nuclei, unlike [[fe56]] and [[pb]] (2301.02272).
- Source: [[source/2301.02272]].

### ν_μ CCQE-like at MINERvA (2503.15047)

- Passive carbon target at [[minerva]], NuMI ME beam (⟨E_ν⟩ ∼ 6 GeV); 2,255
  events after cuts, 54% purity (2503.15047). Channel: [[ccqe]]. Measured vs
  [[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]] and muon/proton kinematics.
- Cross-section **ratio to [[ch]] per neutron is consistent with unity** (as for
  [[o16]] water) — simple neutron-number scaling holds for this light nucleus,
  unlike [[fe56]] and [[pb]] where [[fsi]] drives deviations (2503.15047).
- Source: [[source/2503.15047]].

### (e,e'p) quasi-elastic at JLab Hall C (nucl-ex/0303011)

- ≈ 200 mg/cm² solid target (thickness known to ≈ 0.1%) at [[jlab-hall-c]]
  (nucl-ex/0303011). Channel: [[em-qe]].
- Nuclear [[nuclear-transparency]] (Table III; statistical errors in
  parentheses) (nucl-ex/0303011):
  - Q² = 0.64 (θ_e forward) 0.61(0.02); (θ_e backward) 0.64(0.02)
  - Q² = 1.28: 0.60(0.02)
  - Q² = 1.80 (θ_e forward) 0.57(0.01); Q² = 1.83 (θ_e backward) 0.59(0.01)
  - Q² = 3.25: 0.58(0.02)
- Transparency PWIA correlation factor applied: 1.11 ± 0.03 (nucl-ex/0303011).
- [[spectral-function]] extracted via shell-region E_m cuts: p-shell
  10 < E_m < 25 MeV, s-shell 30 < E_m < 50 MeV (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Considerable excess transverse strength seen at Q² = 0.64 (GeV/c)², much
  reduced at 1.8 (GeV/c)² ([[longitudinal-transverse-separation]])
  (nucl-ex/0303011).

Source: [[source/nucl-ex_0303011]].
