# Lead (Pb)

Heavy nuclear target. At [[minerva]] it is a passive lead target listed with
Z = 82, N = 124 in the statistics table (2503.15047).

## Measurements

### ν_μ CCQE-like vs muon kinematics at MINERvA (2301.02272)

- Passive lead target at [[minerva]], NuMI ME beam (peak E_ν ≈ 6.5 GeV);
  124,000 selected interactions after background subtraction — the highest-
  statistics nuclear target; background 33–45% across nuclei, lowest in Pb
  (2301.02272). Channel: [[ccqe]] (muon-only signal, **no proton requirement**).
- **Primary headline result.** The Pb/CH cross-section ratio per nucleon is
  **always above unity** with a characteristic shape in transverse muon
  momentum P_T that **evolves slowly with longitudinal momentum P_∥**; the ratio
  is constant vs P_∥ within uncertainties above P_∥ = 4.5 GeV/c (2301.02272).
  Contrast the lighter targets, whose ratios to [[ch]] stay near unity
  ([[c12]], [[o16]]) (2301.02272).
- **Both the overall level and the P_T shape for Pb (and [[fe56]]) are not
  reproduced by current neutrino event generators** (2301.02272) — see
  [[genie]]/[[nuwro]]/[[gibuu]]. The data prefer GENIE's hA [[fsi]] model over
  hN, and in [[nuwro]] prefer the [[spectral-function]] over LFG; the GiBUU
  comparison hints cascade-type models better capture pion intranuclear
  absorption in heavy nuclei (2301.02272).
- Evidence of A-scaling that is **not constant over the momentum transferred to
  the nucleus** and not predicted by any generator considered (2301.02272).

Source: [[source/2301.02272]].

### ν_μ CCQE-like at MINERvA (2503.15047)

- Passive lead target at [[minerva]]; 8,660 events after cuts, 55% purity;
  fiducial mass uncertainty < 1% (2503.15047). Channel: [[ccqe]].
- **Strongest A-dependence of the sample.** The δP_T distribution shifts to
  higher values than any lighter target, and the data shift is even larger than
  the [[genie]] "MINERvA tune" predicts — a clear [[fsi]] signature
  ([[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]]) (2503.15047).
- Cross-section **ratio to [[ch]] per neutron deviates clearly from unity**
  (unlike [[c12]] and [[o16]]); simple neutron-number scaling breaks down for
  the heaviest nucleus (2503.15047).
- In the high-FSI regions the model spread covers the data but agreement
  degrades: [[neut]] over-predicts (most for large targets), hN [[genie]]
  approaches NEUT, hA GENIE and [[nuwro]] under-predict, [[gibuu]] is most
  consistent (2503.15047).

Source: [[source/2503.15047]].
