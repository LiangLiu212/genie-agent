# Oxygen-16 (¹⁶O) / water target

Light nuclear target. At [[minerva]] it is realized as a passive **water (H₂O)**
target in the nuclear-target region; cross sections are reported per nucleon
(2503.15047).

## Measurements

### ν_μ CCQE-like vs muon kinematics at MINERvA (2301.02272)

- Passive water target at [[minerva]], NuMI ME beam (peak E_ν ≈ 6.5 GeV);
  20,000 selected interactions after background subtraction (2301.02272). The
  exposure was taken half with the water-target balloon empty, half full, to
  measure and subtract non-water backgrounds directly (2301.02272). Channel:
  [[ccqe]] (muon-only signal, no proton requirement).
- Water-to-[[ch]] cross-section ratio shows **no significant deviation from
  unity** and is roughly constant in P_∥, like [[c12]] — simple scaling holds
  for the light nuclei (2301.02272). See [[fsi]].
- Source: [[source/2301.02272]].

### ν_μ CCQE-like at MINERvA (2503.15047)

- Passive water target at [[minerva]]; statistics table lists Z = 10, N = 8
  (i.e. the oxygen nucleus) (2503.15047). Channel: [[ccqe]].
- 1,563 events after cuts, 47% purity — the lowest-statistics target; water mass
  uncertainty ≈ 1% (2503.15047).
- Cross-section **ratio to [[ch]] per neutron is consistent with unity**, like
  [[c12]] — simple neutron-number scaling works for these smaller nuclei, in
  contrast to [[fe56]] and [[pb]] where [[fsi]] drives deviations (2503.15047).
- Measured vs [[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]] variables and muon/proton
  kinematics.

Source: [[source/2503.15047]].
