# GiBUU

Transport-theory-based neutrino event generator. Compared against the MINERvA
A-dependence data ([[ccqe]]) as **GiBUU release 2019 patch 8** (configurations
T0 and T1), run via NUISANCE (2503.15047).

## In the comparison (2503.15047)

GiBUU is the **most consistent** generator across targets and
[[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]] variables, including the high-[[fsi]] regions
where [[neut]] over-predicts and hA [[genie]] / [[nuwro]] under-predict
(2503.15047). Its coupled transport treatment of [[fsi]] is the likely reason it
tracks the A-dependence better than the cascade-based generators
(2503.15047). Source: [[source/2503.15047]].
