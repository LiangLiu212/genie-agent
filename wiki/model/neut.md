# NEUT

Neutrino event generator (T2K's primary generator). Compared against the MINERvA
A-dependence data ([[ccqe]]) as **NEUT 5.4.1 with a local Fermi gas (LFG)**
nuclear model, run via NUISANCE (2503.15047).

## In the comparison (2503.15047)

NEUT tends to **over-predict** the CCQE-like cross sections, especially on the
large targets ([[fe56]], [[pb]]) and in the high-[[fsi]]
[[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]] regions; the hN variants of [[genie]]
approach NEUT there, while hA GENIE and [[nuwro]] sit below the data and
[[gibuu]] is the most consistent (2503.15047). Source: [[source/2503.15047]].
