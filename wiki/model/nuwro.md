# NuWro

Neutrino event generator (Wrocław). Compared against the MINERvA A-dependence
data ([[ccqe]]) as **NuWro 19.02** with two nuclear models — local Fermi gas
(LFG) and spectral function (SF) — run via NUISANCE (2503.15047).

## In the comparison (2503.15047)

NuWro (like the hA variants of [[genie]]) tends to **under-predict** the
CCQE-like cross sections in the high-[[fsi]]
[[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]] tails, where [[neut]] over-predicts and
[[gibuu]] is most consistent (2503.15047). Source: [[source/2503.15047]].
