# NuWro

Neutrino event generator (Wrocław). Compared against the MINERvA A-dependence
data ([[ccqe]]) as **NuWro 19.02** with two nuclear models — local Fermi gas
(LFG) and spectral function (SF) — run via NUISANCE (2503.15047).

## In the comparisons

- **TKI analysis (2503.15047):** NuWro (like the hA variants of [[genie]]) tends
  to **under-predict** the CCQE-like cross sections in the high-[[fsi]]
  [[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]] tails, where [[neut]] over-predicts and
  [[gibuu]] is most consistent (2503.15047).
- **Muon-kinematics analysis (2301.02272):** the data **prefer the
  [[spectral-function]] (SF) over LFG** within NuWro — i.e. a more realistic
  initial-state momentum distribution improves agreement with the muon-kinematics
  cross sections (2301.02272).
