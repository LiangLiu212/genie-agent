# Measurement of the A dependence of the ν_μ CCQE-like cross section vs muon and proton kinematics at ⟨E_ν⟩ ∼ 6 GeV

The MINERvA Collaboration (J. Kleykamp et al.) (2503.15047).

**Scope.** ν_μ charged-current quasielastic-like ([[ccqe]]) scattering in the
NuMI medium-energy beam at [[minerva]], ⟨E_ν⟩ ∼ 6 GeV, 10.61 × 10²⁰ POT
(2012–2019) (2503.15047). Differential cross sections measured **simultaneously**
on [[c12]], [[ch]] (scintillator), [[o16]] (water), [[fe56]] and [[pb]] in the
same detector and flux, as functions of transverse-kinematic-imbalance variables
([[transverse-kinematic-imbalance]]) and muon/proton kinematics (2503.15047).
Headline: the first simultaneous ν_μ CCQE-like TKI cross sections across these
five targets; δP_T shifts to higher values with increasing A (a [[fsi]]
signature), and ratios to CH per neutron deviate from unity for Fe and Pb
(2503.15047).
