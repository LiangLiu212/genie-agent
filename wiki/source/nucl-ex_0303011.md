# A Study of the Quasi-elastic (e,e'p) Reaction on ¹²C, ⁵⁶Fe and ¹⁹⁷Au

D. Dutta, D. van Westrum, et al. (Jefferson Lab Hall C, experiment E91-013) (nucl-ex/0303011).

**Scope.** Continuous CEBAF electron beam at [[jlab-hall-c]]; coincidence (e,e'p)
quasi-elastic knockout ([[em-qe]]) on [[c12]], [[fe56]] and [[au197]] over
Q² = 0.64–3.25 (GeV/c)² (nucl-ex/0303011). Headline results: nuclear
[[nuclear-transparency]] and radiation-corrected [[spectral-function]]s for all
three nuclei, plus [[longitudinal-transverse-separation]]s at Q² = 0.64 and
1.8 (GeV/c)² (the Fe and Au L–T separations being the first such data on medium
and heavy nuclei) (nucl-ex/0303011).
