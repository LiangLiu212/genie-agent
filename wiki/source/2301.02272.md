# Simultaneous measurement of ν_μ quasielastic-like cross sections on CH, C, H₂O, Fe, and Pb as a function of muon kinematics at MINERvA

The MINERvA Collaboration (J. Kleykamp et al.) (2301.02272).

**Scope.** ν_μ charged-current quasielastic-like ([[ccqe]]) scattering in the
NuMI medium-energy beam at [[minerva]], peak E_ν ≈ 6.5 GeV, 10.61 × 10²⁰ POT
(2301.02272). Cross sections measured **simultaneously** on [[ch]] (scintillator),
[[c12]], [[o16]] (water), [[fe56]] and [[pb]] in one detector/flux, double-
differential in **longitudinal (P_∥) and transverse (P_T) muon momentum**
(2301.02272). Headline: the Pb/CH per-nucleon ratio is always above unity with a
characteristic P_T shape that evolves slowly in P_∥; neither overall level nor
P_T shape for Pb and Fe is reproduced by current generators — evidence for an
A-scaling that varies with momentum transfer (2301.02272).

This is the **muon-kinematics companion** to the TKI measurement
[[source/2503.15047]] (same author, beam, exposure and targets); note the
signal definitions differ — this analysis places **no requirement on protons**
(any number of nucleons), whereas 2503.15047 requires a leading proton.
