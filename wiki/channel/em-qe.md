# (e,e'p) quasi-elastic knockout

Electromagnetic quasi-elastic single-nucleon knockout: an electron scatters
from a single bound proton, and the proton is detected in coincidence with the
scattered electron (nucl-ex/0303011). The electron-scattering analogue of
neutrino [[ccqe]] on the proton; a clean probe of nuclear structure
([[spectral-function]], [[missing-energy-momentum]]) and of final-state
interactions ([[nuclear-transparency]]).

## Kinematics (nucl-ex/0303011)

- Missing energy: E_m = ω − T_p′ − T_{A−1} (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Missing momentum: p⃗_m = p⃗_p′ − q⃗ (nucl-ex/0303011).
- See [[missing-energy-momentum]] for the integration windows used.

## Cross section (PWIA) (nucl-ex/0303011)

In PWIA the sixfold coincidence cross section factorizes into the off-shell e-p
cross section times the nuclear [[spectral-function]], with longitudinal,
transverse and interference response functions W_L, W_T, W_LT, W_TT
(nucl-ex/0303011):

```
d⁶σ/(dE_e′ dΩ_e′ dE_p′ dΩ_p′) = p′ E_p′ σ_Mott ·
  [ λ² W_L + (λ/2 + tan²(θ/2)) W_T
    + λ(λ + tan²(θ/2))^{1/2} W_LT cos φ + (λ/2) W_TT cos 2φ ]
```

with λ = Q²/|q⃗|² (nucl-ex/0303011). The off-shell e-p cross section uses the
deForest σ_cc1 prescription (nucl-ex/0303011).

## Measurements in this wiki

- JLab Hall C E91-013 on [[c12]], [[fe56]], [[au197]], Q² = 0.64–3.25 (GeV/c)²
  (nucl-ex/0303011); see [[source/nucl-ex_0303011]]. Key findings:
  [[nuclear-transparency]] vs Q², extracted [[spectral-function]]s, and
  [[longitudinal-transverse-separation]]s showing excess transverse strength at
  low Q² (nucl-ex/0303011).
