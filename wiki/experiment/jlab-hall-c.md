# Jefferson Lab Hall C

Experimental hall at Jefferson Lab fed by the continuous CEBAF electron beam.

## Beam (nucl-ex/0303011)

- Continuous CEBAF electron beam; experiment E91-013 was a commissioning run
  (1995–1996) (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Beam energies: nominal E_e = (0.8N + 0.045) GeV with N = 1–4 passes; settings
  used were 0.845, 1.645, 2.445 and 3.245 GeV (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Absolute beam-energy calibration by two independent methods (dispersion
  calibration via the ¹²C 4.43891 ± 0.00031 MeV excited state on a BeO target,
  and a diffraction-minimum method using the ¹²C first excited state at
  Q² = 0.129 ± 0.0006 (GeV/c)²); the two agreed to 1 part in 2000, with absolute
  energy accuracy believed to be 10⁻³ (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Beam current 10–60 μA, monitored by 3 microwave cavities calibrated against an
  Unser cavity; overall accuracy ±1% (nucl-ex/0303011).

## Spectrometers (nucl-ex/0303011)

Coincidence measurement: the **HMS** (High Momentum Spectrometer) detected
electrons and the **SOS** (Short Orbit Spectrometer) detected protons, except at
the highest Q² = 3.25 (GeV/c)² where the roles were reversed (nucl-ex/0303011).

- **HMS** — 25° vertical-bend superconducting QQQD; rotation 12.5°–90°; max
  central momentum 7.3 GeV/c (highest used 2.6 GeV/c); usable momentum bite ≈ 20%;
  momentum resolution (σ) < 1.4 × 10⁻³; solid angle 8.1 msr (point target),
  6.8 msr with a 6.35 cm HEAVYMET collimator (nucl-ex/0303011).
- **SOS** — QDD̄, vertical net bend 18°, path length 11 m; rotation 13.1°–168.4°;
  max central momentum 1.8 GeV/c, nominal momentum bite 40%; momentum resolution
  (σ) < 1.0 × 10⁻³; solid angle ≈ 9 msr (point target), 7.5 msr with collimator
  (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Detector stacks (both arms): drift chambers → hodoscope pair → gas Čerenkov →
  hodoscope pair → lead-glass calorimeter; wire-chamber tracking efficiency
  typically > 97%, known to better than 1% (nucl-ex/0303011).
- Calibration data taken with the 4.0 cm Hall C cryogenic (liquid hydrogen)
  target (nucl-ex/0303011).
