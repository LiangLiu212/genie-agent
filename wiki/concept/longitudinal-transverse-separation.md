# Longitudinal–Transverse (L–T) separation

A Rosenbluth-style separation of the (e,e'p) ([[em-qe]]) cross section into its
longitudinal (W_L) and transverse (W_T) response functions by measuring at fixed
Q² and varying the virtual-photon polarization ε. Isolates the longitudinal and
transverse pieces of the [[spectral-function]].

## Results (nucl-ex/0303011)

JLab Hall C E91-013 performed L–T separations at Q² = 0.64 and 1.8 (GeV/c)² on
[[c12]], [[fe56]] and [[au197]]; the iron and gold separations are "the first
such data on medium and heavy nuclei" (nucl-ex/0303011). To suppress the W_LT and
W_TT interference terms the separations were restricted to |p_m| < 80 MeV/c
([[missing-energy-momentum]]) (nucl-ex/0303011).

Headline finding (verbatim): "In all three targets the measured spectral
functions show considerable excess transverse strength at Q² = 0.64 (GeV/c)²,
which is much reduced at 1.8 (GeV/c)²" (nucl-ex/0303011). This excess transverse
strength is a signature of contributions beyond single-nucleon knockout (e.g.
two-body currents / [[mec-2p2h]]) at low Q² (nucl-ex/0303011).
