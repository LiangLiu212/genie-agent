# Nuclear transparency

The probability that a struck nucleon escapes the nucleus without re-interacting
— a direct measure of final-state interactions ([[fsi]]). For (e,e'p)
([[em-qe]]) it is defined as the ratio of the measured e-p coincidence yield to
the PWIA-predicted yield (nucl-ex/0303011):

```
T = (measured e-p coincidence yield, |p_m| ≤ 300 MeV/c, E_m ≤ 80 MeV) / (PWIA-predicted yield)
```

PWIA correlation factors are applied: 1.11 ± 0.03 ([[c12]]), 1.26 ± 0.08
([[fe56]]), 1.32 ± 0.08 ([[au197]]) (nucl-ex/0303011).

## Measurements (nucl-ex/0303011)

JLab Hall C E91-013, (e,e'p) on C, Fe, Au, Q² = 0.64–3.25 (GeV/c)²
([[source/nucl-ex_0303011]]). Table III (statistical errors in parentheses):

| Q² (GeV/c)² | carbon | iron | gold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.64 (θ_e forward) | 0.61(0.02) | 0.47(0.01) | 0.38(0.01) |
| 0.64 (θ_e backward) | 0.64(0.02) | 0.54(0.01) | 0.43(0.01) |
| 1.28 | 0.60(0.02) | 0.44(0.01) | 0.32(0.01) |
| 1.80 (θ_e forward) | 0.57(0.01) | 0.40(0.01) | 0.29(0.01) |
| 1.83 (θ_e backward) | 0.59(0.01) | 0.44(0.01) | — |
| 3.25 | 0.58(0.02) | 0.42(0.01) | 0.28(0.01) |

Transparency falls with increasing nuclear mass (C > Fe > Au) and is roughly
flat / slowly varying in Q² over this range (nucl-ex/0303011).

## Uncertainties (nucl-ex/0303011)

- Statistical ~0.01, never > 0.02.
- Systematic ≈ 2.5% overall, ≈ 2% point-to-point.
- Model dependence (radiative corrections, off-shell e-p cross section,
  correlation corrections) summed in quadrature ≈ 5% (C) and ≈ 8% (Fe, Au);
  relative same-target uncertainties < 5% (nucl-ex/0303011).
