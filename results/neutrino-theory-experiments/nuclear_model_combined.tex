\caption{Effect of the nuclear ground-state model on the MicroBooNE CC1$\mu$1p / CC1$\mu$Np argon cross sections with the LQCD axial form factor and hA2018 FSI held fixed: spectral function (blue) versus Local Fermi Gas (orange), compared with MicroBooNE data (black points). Panels: (a) $\delta p_T$ for $\delta\alpha_T<45^\circ$ and (b) for $135^\circ<\delta\alpha_T<180^\circ$ (two slices of the 2D $\delta p_T$--$\delta\alpha_T$ measurement); (c) $\delta p_T$; (d) $\delta\alpha_T$; (e) $p_\mu$; (f) $p_p$. With hA2018 the two ground states give very similar, good agreement with the data (small $\Delta\chi^2/{\rm ndf}$ in every observable; see Table~\ref{tab:chisq_model_lqcd_ha2018}).}
